# Governance and maintenance

Read-only Alexa energy reports, the standalone report CLI, optional account linking and their deployment tools.

The primary maintainer is the `4alvit` account. The maintainer owns project scope, review decisions, dependency maintenance, vulnerability handling and release acceptance. Contributors propose issues, documentation, tests and pull requests. Automation supplies checks and review evidence; it does not replace maintainer responsibility or authorize a deployment.

Use public issues for ordinary reports and design proposals, and pull requests for reviewed changes. Record compatibility effects, tests and the reason for a decision. Participants can disagree respectfully and request reconsideration with new evidence. Changes follow current protected-branch checks and review rules; this policy grants no administrator bypass.

The [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) governs participation. Confidential technical reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Release acceptance follows [RELEASING.md](RELEASING.md).

## Continuity limitation

There is one primary maintainer, with no designated backup person or tested arrangement that restores repository, CI and release access within one week. Public source and bot access do not establish that guarantee. OpenSSF Silver access continuity and a bus factor of two remain unmet until a real recovery arrangement is authorized and tested.

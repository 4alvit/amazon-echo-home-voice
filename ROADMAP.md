# Roadmap

This roadmap covers 16 October 2026 through at least 16 October 2027. The priorities for that year are:

- Keep the standalone and voice report contracts aligned; preserve household isolation and deadline tests.
- Extend systematic branch/statement coverage across application, deployment and release tooling.
- Verify additional complete cloud ingress profiles independently before extending cryptographic claims.

For all work, preserve compatibility, add failure-path regressions and document operational limits. Establish and test maintainer-access continuity when a real backup arrangement exists. These are plans, not delivered features or a promise of dates. Propose and discuss changes through GitHub issues and pull requests.

## Non-goals for this period

The project will not become a general voice assistant, bypass Alexa account/signature verification, expose household write controls, or claim Amazon certification without completing its separate process.
